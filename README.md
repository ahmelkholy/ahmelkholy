# Ahmed M. Elkholy

**Electrical power engineer · Researcher · Engineering software developer**

I am a researcher in the Industrial Electronics Department at Moscow Power Engineering Institute (MPEI) and an Assistant Lecturer at Tanta University. My PhD focuses on shunt regulation in distribution networks with unbalanced loads; completion is expected in December 2026.

My work includes transformer commissioning tests and modelling, reactive-power compensation, power-system studies and engineering specifications. I also develop software in Julia and Python.

[Website and articles](https://ahmelkholy.github.io/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmelkholy/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KbZs8_AAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-1834-1175) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ahmelkholy)

## Engineering work

- **MPEI:** Zigzag-transformer commissioning tests and measurement-based MATLAB/Simulink models; modelling and performance assessment of a departmental 50 kvar, 0.4 kV thyristor-switched SVC; simulation of shunt regulators and coordinated compensator control.
- **Independent studies:** ETAP load-flow, short-circuit, arc-flash and protection-setting studies for industrial and PV projects, including capacitor compensation and battery-source scenarios. Reports prepared for client review.
- **Power Consult:** Project-based contributions to utility equipment specifications and inspection/test plans, currently under committee review.
- **Tanta University:** Tutorials, laboratory teaching, assessment and student project supervision.
