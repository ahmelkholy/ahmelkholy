## Projects

### AIMORA · Power-system analysis in Julia

I lead development of AIMORA. Current work covers EMT simulation, line/cable and transformer parameters, project/study interfaces and numerical tests. Broader study modules and the desktop client are planned.

**[Explore AIMORA →](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev)** · [AIMORA.jl](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORA.jl) · [Platform](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORAPlatform) · [Resources](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORAResources)

### EV charging · Corridor planning

As lead author and software developer, I developed Python genetic-algorithm software to locate and size fast chargers under traffic, grid, storage and renewable-energy constraints. The published study evaluates five scenarios over a 669 km motorway corridor, with **50 runs in total**.

**[Read the paper →](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsr.2026.113505)**

## Engineering experience

**Commissioning and modelling · Power-system studies · Specifications · Teaching**

<details>
<summary><strong>MPEI — transformer commissioning, SVC analysis and shunt regulation</strong></summary>

**Researcher / R&D Engineer · Industrial Electronics Department · October 2023–present**

- Participated in zigzag-transformer commissioning tests and developed measurement-based variable-impedance models in MATLAB/Simulink, comparing predictions with measured system behaviour at 50 Hz.
- Contributed to modelling and performance assessment of a departmental **50 kvar, 0.4 kV thyristor-switched SVC**, using R to analyse two months of operating data.
- Developed shunt-regulator models, phase-power calculations and genetic-algorithm coordination of two compensators on the IEEE 13-bus feeder in simulation.

[Zigzag paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prime.2025.101135) · [SVC paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsr.2024.111187)
